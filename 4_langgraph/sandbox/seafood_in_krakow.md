# Best Seafood Restaurants in Kraków

Here are five of the best seafood restaurants in Kraków, complete with addresses and price ranges to help you make an informed choice:

1. **Ristorante Santa Caterina**  
   - **Address:** Karmelicka 36, 31-128 Kraków, Poland  
   - **Price Range:** PLN 50 - 120  
   - **Description:** Known for its exquisite Italian seafood dishes, Ristorante Santa Caterina is located in the heart of the Old Town, just 5 minutes from the main square. The menu features a blend of traditional recipes and modern gastronomy, providing a fine dining experience.

2. **Broo Seafood & Bar**  
   - **Address:** Ulica św. Katarzyny 4, 31-063 Kraków, Poland  
   - **Price Range:** PLN 40 - 60  
   - **Description:** This casual dining restaurant offers a vibrant atmosphere and focuses on quick yet delicious seafood options, including fish and chips. It's a great spot for those looking for quality seafood without the formal setting.

3. **Farina Restaurant**  
   - **Address:** Św. Marka 16, 31-018 Kraków, Poland  
   - **Price Range:** PLN 25 - 100  
   - **Description:** Situated near the Old Town, Farina is famous for its Italian-inspired seafood dishes, prepared with fresh ingredients. The atmosphere is warm and inviting, making it an excellent place for a relaxed meal.

4. **Trzy Rybki**  
   - **Address:** Szczepańska 5, 31-011 Kraków, Poland  
   - **Price Range:** PLN 50 - 90  
   - **Description:** This restaurant specializes in a variety of fish dishes and offers an innovative menu that changes seasonally. It provides a cozy environment, perfect for a special dining experience.

5. **Albertina Restaurant & Wine**  
   - **Address:** Dominikańska 3, 31-043 Kraków, Poland  
   - **Price Range:** PLN 100 - 200  
   - **Description:** This fine dining establishment combines exquisite seafood with a carefully curated wine selection. With a chic atmosphere and a focus on local ingredients, Albertina is perfect for special occasions and upscale dining for seafood aficionados.

Enjoy your culinary journey through Kraków's seafood scene!
